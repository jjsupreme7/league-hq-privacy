The League

Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Short version: The League HQ is a fantasy-football companion for Sleeper leagues. It uses the minimum account and league data needed to provide the app, does not sell personal data, and does not contain advertising.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, an internal account identifier, and authentication credentials handled by Supabase. The developer cannot read your password.
  • League information: your Sleeper username, league and franchise connection, and public fantasy-football data such as rosters, matchups, transactions, standings, and player statistics.
  • Optional profile content: a profile photo and team customization you choose to save. A device copy may remain cached after sign-out until you remove it or clear the app's data.
  • Feature-usage records: limited records needed to operate and rate-limit AI features, including the member, feature, time, model, and token counts. Ask the Desk conversation text is not retained in this usage ledger.
  • Operational information: service providers may process standard network and security information, such as IP address and request logs, to deliver and protect the service.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate members, connect each member to the correct franchise, sync profile photos, display league analysis, operate trade and Ask the Desk features, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and maintain the security of the app.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. Data is processed only as needed by services that operate the app: Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and edge functions; Sleeper as the source of public league data; Anthropic for requested AI answers and league analysis; Vercel for web hosting where used; and Apple or Google for app distribution and store purchases. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy commitments.

AI processing and your choices

Before using personal AI answers in the updated app, you choose whether to allow your question, recent conversation and relevant Sleeper league context to be sent to Anthropic. Context can include rosters, trades, matchups, team names and manager display names. Do not include sensitive personal information in a question. You can withdraw this permission in Profile → AI privacy; the choice is stored per account on that device, and free league browsing remains available. Existing shared Desk summaries are generated from league information and may be stored for reuse. AI can make mistakes.

Anthropic processes API requests under its commercial service terms and privacy practices. See Anthropic's privacy policy. We do not promise that withdrawing consent removes information already processed by a service provider.

Notifications, saved content and usage

If you enable notifications, we store the device's push token and selected league notification preferences. Apple delivers iPhone notifications. Saved players, followed teams, trade ideas, appearance choices and limited feature-use events help provide and improve the app. You can change notification permission in iPhone Settings. Face ID and device-passcode verification happen on your device; we do not receive or store your biometric data.

Subscriptions

Desk Plus is an optional monthly subscription offered through Apple's in-app purchase system when available. Apple handles payment details; we receive verified purchase identifiers, subscription dates and status, and record AI allowance usage to provide paid access and prevent abuse. We do not receive your full payment-card number. The purchase screen shows the price and included allowance before you subscribe. Cancel or manage your subscription in Apple subscription settings.

Data security and retention

Network traffic is encrypted in transit. Database access is restricted with authentication and row-level authorization. Account and membership data is retained while the account is active. Security, invite-attempt, and AI-usage records may be retained for abuse prevention, auditing, and cost reconciliation. Invite-attempt records are automatically pruned. We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for these purposes.

Your choices and account deletion

You may remove an optional profile photo in the app. To permanently delete your account, open Profile → Member Account → Delete my account and confirm the deletion. If you are the league's only administrator, you must first assign another administrator so the league is not left without one.

If you cannot access the app, email jjgcallen11@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and request account deletion. Account deletion removes the authentication account, linked membership and associated subscription records. Deleting an account or the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it separately in Apple subscription settings to stop renewal. Limited de-identified operational or usage records may remain where needed for security, abuse prevention, and accounting.

Children

The League HQ is not directed to children under 13. The App Store listing provides its content age rating.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when that happens.

Support

For sign-in, Sleeper connections, inaccurate or offensive AI content, subscriptions, or deletion help, email jjgcallen11@gmail.com. Include the app version and a description of the issue; do not send your password or payment-card details. For Apple refunds, use Apple's refund request service.
