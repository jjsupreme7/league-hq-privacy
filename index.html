The League

Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

Short version: The League is a private fantasy-football companion for one league. It uses the minimum account and league data needed to provide the app, does not sell personal data, and does not contain advertising.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, an internal account identifier, and authentication credentials handled by Supabase. The developer cannot read your password.
  • League information: your Sleeper username, league and franchise connection, and public fantasy-football data such as rosters, matchups, transactions, standings, and player statistics.
  • Optional profile content: a profile photo and team customization you choose to save. A device copy may remain cached after sign-out until you remove it or clear the app's data.
  • Feature-usage records: limited records needed to operate and rate-limit AI features, including the member, feature, time, model, and token counts. Ask the Desk conversation text is not retained in this usage ledger.
  • Operational information: service providers may process standard network and security information, such as IP address and request logs, to deliver and protect the service.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate members, connect each member to the correct franchise, sync profile photos, display league analysis, operate trade and Ask the Desk features, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and maintain the security of the app.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. Data is processed only as needed by services that operate the app: Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and edge functions; Sleeper as the source of public league data; Vercel AI Gateway and its configured model provider for requested AI analysis; and Apple or Google for app distribution. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy commitments.

Data security and retention

Network traffic is encrypted in transit. Database access is restricted with authentication and row-level authorization. Account and membership data is retained while the account is active. Security, invite-attempt, and AI-usage records may be retained for abuse prevention, auditing, and cost reconciliation. Invite-attempt records are automatically pruned. We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for these purposes.

Your choices and deletion

You may remove an optional profile photo in the app. You may permanently delete your account from Profile → Member Account → Delete my account. If you are the league's only administrator, you must first assign another administrator so the league is not left without one. Account deletion removes the authentication account and linked membership; limited de-identified operational or usage records may remain where needed for security and accounting.

Children

The League is intended for adults in a private fantasy-football league and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when that happens.
